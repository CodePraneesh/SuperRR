## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

1. **Historical Preservation**: The official Phase 8 submission rankings (`official_top10_batters.csv` and `official_top10_bowlers.csv`) remain completely unmodified. All historical ball-by-ball deliveries, WPA expectations, and CPRI algorithms are strictly frozen.
2. **Active-Player Filter**: To serve dynamic tournament selection needs, a separate **Active Professional T20 Leaderboard** is established that filters confirmed retirees.
3. **Distinction of Retirement Levels**: Retirement is strictly differentiated across three levels: (a) IPL Retirement, (b) International Cricket Retirement, and (c) Total Professional Cricket Retirement.
4. **Unsold / Uncontracted Safeguard**: In accordance with Task 5, players are **not** classified as retired merely because they were released, unsold, or temporarily absent from an IPL squad.
